How it works

How to use an Alibarbar Ingot 9000

Three steps. No buttons, no cable, no refill. Opening it means unboxing — not cracking the casing.

  1. 1

    Unbox the Alibarbar Ingot 9000

    Open the sealed retail pack. Take off the mouthpiece cap or sticker, and any film over the air intake. Leave the metal body closed — it is a sealed disposable, not a refillable pod.

  2. 2

    Inhale to activate

    There are no buttons, no charger and no cable. The Alibarbar Ingot 9000 arrives pre-filled and pre-charged. Put the mouthpiece to your lips and take a short, gentle draw.

  3. 3

    Watch the LED

    The smart display shows remaining battery and e-liquid. When either reads empty, or vapour drops off, the device is finished. Replace it — do not try to charge it.

What is in the box

  • One sealed Alibarbar Ingot 9000, pre-filled and pre-charged
  • A mouthpiece cap or sticker, and sometimes a film over the air intake — remove these before the first draw
  • No USB cable, charger, spare coil or refill bottle

Can you charge an Alibarbar Ingot 9000?

No. The Alibarbar Ingot 9000 is a non-rechargeable disposable. The 2350mAh battery is built in and sized for the 22ml tank, so there is no USB-C port, no cable in the box, and no safe way to top it up. When the smart LED shows the battery or e-liquid is finished, or vapour drops off, replace the device and recycle the old one at an e-waste or battery drop-off. Do not open the casing, and do not follow videos that wire a charger to a sealed stick.

How long it lasts & how many puffs

  • Rated for up to 9000 puffs — real totals depend on puff length and draw style; treat 9000 as an upper bound.
  • Average use (~300 puffs a day): about 3–4 weeks per device.
  • Light use (~150 a day): toward 6 weeks. Heavy use (~600 a day): about 2 weeks.
  • Shorter, gentler puffs and cool storage help a device last longer.

What the LED means

  • The smart LED shows two readings: remaining battery and remaining e-liquid — check both.
  • E-liquid empty = the device is finished, even if some battery is left.
  • Battery empty and vapour dropping = the device is finished.
  • Flashing with no vapour, weak vapour or a burnt taste are end-of-life signals.
  • It is non-rechargeable: replace the device, do not try to charge it.
